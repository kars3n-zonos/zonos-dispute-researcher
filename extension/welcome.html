Dispute Researcher is installed

Shared notes, saved Claude answers and merchant research on every duty dispute.

  1. Open a duty dispute on the brokerage dashboard.
  2. Click Notes and Research in the bottom-right corner. Shared notes turn on by themselves through your dashboard sign-in.
  3. Add the Entry Assistant token once for HTS lookups: open Settings at the bottom of the panel, paste the token (ask whoever shared this extension with you), then click Save and Test.
  4. Optional: pin it. Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to Zonos Dispute Researcher. Its toolbar button opens the panel too.

Using the Tampermonkey version as well? It switches itself off now that the extension is here, so you won't get two panels. You can remove it from Tampermonkey whenever you like.

Open in Claude opens claude.ai in a new tab with the prompt and the documents filled in. Nothing is sent until you press Enter there.
